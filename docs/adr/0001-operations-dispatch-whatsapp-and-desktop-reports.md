# Dual Operations Dispatch: Desktop Reports vs Smartphone WhatsApp Channel

Office administrative staff operate on desktop and laptop computers, whereas temple operations staff (Archakas/Priests and kitchen cooks) communicate exclusively via smartphones on WhatsApp.

We decided to build two distinct operational output channels in the reporting engine:
1. **Office Channel (Desktop)**: Interactive drill-down tables, print stylesheets, and CSV/Excel data exports for financial auditing and administrative management.
2. **Operations Dispatch Channel (Smartphones)**: 1-click formatted Kannada text summaries directly shareable via `wa.me/` links alongside mobile-optimized single-page PDFs for daily Priest Sankalpa lists and Kitchen Hastodaka counts, avoiding login requirements on smartphones.
