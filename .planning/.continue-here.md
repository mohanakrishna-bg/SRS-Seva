<decisions_made>
- 3 Interactive System Roles:
  * `Office Admin` (`admin`): Full administrative authority, `can_cancel_receipts = true`, fiscal date locks, user admin, dispatch exports.
  * `Counter Cashier` (`cashier`): Front desk seva booking, devotee directory, receipt printing, cash drawer. `can_cancel_receipts = false`.
  * `Trustee` (`trustee`): Management committee governance, strict read-only across all modules, `can_export_financial_reports = true`.
- 3 Physical Domain Stakeholders (Zero direct system logins):
  * `Archaka (Priest)`: Physical ritual execution, receives `SankalpaList` (PII masked). Sevas completed via `DeemedFulfillment`.
  * `Kitchen Supervisor (Mahanasa Adhikari)`: Receives `HastodakaCount` report breaking down Dine-in + Parcels + Total meal preparation.
  * `Auditor (Chartered Accountant)`: Receives statutory trial balances, vouchers, and immutable `AuditTrail` packs via export/WhatsApp.
- Advance Parcel Hastodaka:
  * Charged at standard Hastodaka rate (`foodServiceRate`).
  * Total Hastodaka = `(familyMembers + parcelCount) * rate`.
  * Attached strictly to the primary seva line in multi-seva cart to prevent multi-allocation inflation.
</decisions_made>
